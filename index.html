Hello and Namaste!

I am a PhD candidate in Agricultural and Applied Economics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, originally from Nepal. My main research studies the political economy of government advertising. Governments in many developing countries are among the largest buyers of media space, which gives them influence over both voters and the outlets that carry their messages. I examine how this spending affects elections and media markets, and what it reveals about how public resources are used and monitored.
